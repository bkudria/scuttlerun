# Code of Conduct

scuttlerun is a small project, but everyone interacting with it — in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other space — is expected to act with respect and good faith.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Assume good intent; ask for clarification before assuming the worst.
- Welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the maintainer via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/bkudria/scuttlerun/security/advisories) or by opening a private issue. Reports will be reviewed and addressed in good faith.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warnings or removal from project spaces.
